# Privacy Policy — Heard

_Last updated: October 6, 2026_

Heard runs entirely on your machine and has no backend.

- **What it fetches:** only the public feedback sources you approve (RSS/Atom feeds, App Store JSON, canonical JSON endpoints). It does not use sign-ins, cookies or authenticated APIs.
- **What it sends:** nothing. Heard never transmits your data, files or credentials to the author or any third party.
- **What it stores:** collected records and reports (`records.json`, `report.html`, CSV/JSON exports) are written only to your local run directory. Author fields are dropped on collection, but review text can still contain names or handles that reviewers wrote publicly.
- **Retention:** the author collects and retains nothing. You control, and can delete, all output files.
- **Model usage:** analysis runs on the model of the host you use (e.g. Claude), under that host's own terms and privacy policy.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/codingyogini/heard-product-feedback/issues
